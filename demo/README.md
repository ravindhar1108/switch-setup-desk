## Demo Video Guidelines

When recording the submission video:
1. **Target Duration**: Between 2 and 3 minutes.
2. **Key Narrative**:
   - Begin with the developer frustration of broken open-source setups and the personal motivation behind Setup Desk.
   - Show how a request is triggered from Slack.
   - Highlight the **three specialized agents** orchestrated by **Switch**.
   - Focus on the **Self-Healing Loop**: showcase a real setup failure, the diagnosis by Repair, the project-local fix, and Setup's successful verification retry.
   - Conclude with the generated `SETUP.md` deliverable.
3. **Pacing**: Speak clearly and naturally, letting the multi-agent transitions be visible in Slack and the Switch Console.
